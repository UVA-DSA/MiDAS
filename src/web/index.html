Guideline

Do not use spaces, “.”, “/”, or whitespaces in any of the inputs.

Subject:Person using the system

Task:Type of exercise beign recorded

Rate:Frequency(Hz) as a float value between 12 and 255. Any rate value above 120 might lead to less accurate results.

Range:Maximum recordable range (in). Lower ranges increase accuracy.

Hemisphere: Default position front is the way the xyz axes are labled on

Filter: Filtering out a wide or narrow range. Narrower ranges lead to faster computation time.
